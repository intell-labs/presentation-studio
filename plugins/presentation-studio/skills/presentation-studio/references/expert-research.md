# Expert perspective and research

## Select the perspective

Infer the professional perspective required by the decision, not merely the industry topic.

Examples:

| Situation | Primary perspective | Audience-side perspective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Financing | CFO or finance director | Credit analyst or risk committee |
| Board decision | CEO or strategy director | Board member or investor |
| Contract or regulation | Legal director | Regulator, counsel, or procurement |
| Commercial proposal | Sales director | Buyer and economic decision-maker |
| Technology | Product or engineering leader | Technical evaluator and business owner |
| Training | Instructional designer | Learner and manager |
| Research | Subject researcher | Reviewer or policy decision-maker |

State the recommendation in one sentence and ask the user to confirm or add a specialty.

## Gap analysis

Before research, list:

- known facts;
- missing evidence;
- unstable facts needing current verification;
- calculations required;
- claims likely to be challenged;
- confidential information that must not be searched or disclosed.

## Research standards

- Prefer primary and official sources.
- Use current sources for changing facts.
- Use domain-specific authoritative sources for medical, legal, financial, regulatory, or technical claims.
- Record source URL, publisher, publication date, event or effective date, supported claim, and confidence.
- Clearly mark inference.
- Do not fabricate numbers to complete a visual.
- Ask the user when evidence cannot be obtained responsibly.

## Expert recommendations

Do not merely summarize sources. Evaluate them from the confirmed role and explain:

- what matters to the audience;
- what weakens the argument;
- what additional evidence would improve it;
- what should be omitted;
- what decision language is defensible.

## Final content review

Present facts, calculations, assumptions, recommendations, and unresolved questions separately. Ask the user to confirm or edit the final content before visual exploration.
