- Editorial content generated for the presentation belongs to the user to the extent permitted by applicable law and platform terms.
- Third-party logos, fonts, images, data, and other resources remain subject to their own licenses, permissions, and trademark rules.
- Never imply that Presentation Studio grants rights to material it did not create or own.

## Presentation Studio runtime

The HTML runtime code is licensed under Apache-2.0. A generated presentation may contain both user-owned content and the Apache-2.0 runtime. Preserve the supplied generator metadata, About panel, and attribution when distributing the runtime or a derivative. Include the Apache license and `NOTICE` in source distributions or another customary location allowed by the license.

Do not add a permanent intell labs logo or watermark to slides by default. Attribution belongs in metadata, the discreet About panel, and accompanying notices so it does not compete with the presenter's brand.

## Brand boundaries

The Apache license does not grant trademark rights. Read `TRADEMARKS.md` in this skill package before branding a fork or commercial wrapper. Accurate nominative statements such as “Built with Presentation Studio by intell labs” are permitted; modified distributions must not imply official endorsement.

## Delivery checklist

- Keep `<meta name="generator" content="Presentation Studio by intell labs">`.
- Keep `<meta name="generator-url" content="https://github.com/intell-labs/presentation-studio">`.
- Keep the About panel and its Apache-2.0 disclosure.
- Record third-party asset origins and constraints in the project contract.
- Never present a client's confidential information in public metadata.
